# Spatial Accessibility to Major Healthcare Facilities in Ada East District, Ghana

## Project Overview

This undergraduate research investigated spatial accessibility to major healthcare facilities in Ada East District, Ghana, with particular attention to communities whose journeys depend on both road networks and boat crossings.

## Research Objectives

* Examine spatial differences in access to major healthcare facilities.
* Integrate boat crossings with existing road networks for more realistic accessibility modelling.
* Assess travel-time differences between mainland, riverine, and island communities.

## Methodology

* Digitisation of boat crossings and integration with road networks.
* GIS-based network analysis using QGIS.
* Travel-time modelling to assess healthcare accessibility.
* Household surveys involving 70 households across seven communities.
* Comparative analysis of accessibility patterns.

## Key Findings

None of the 23 households surveyed in island and peninsula communities reported a travel time under 30 minutes, compared with 94.7% of mainland households.

## Technical Skills and Tools

* QGIS
* GIS network analysis
* Spatial data processing
* Network-based travel-time modelling
* Household survey data collection and analysis

## Research Significance

The research highlights the importance of incorporating local transport connections into accessibility models, particularly in communities where conventional road-network data do not adequately represent actual travel routes.
